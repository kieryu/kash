# Kash privacy policy (draft)

_Last updated: [date before publishing]. Draft — fill in the contact line and host this page before the Play listing goes live._

## The short version
Kash keeps your data on your phone. We don't have servers, accounts or analytics, so we never see your debts, payments or anything else you type in.

## What Kash stores
Everything you enter — debt nicknames, balances, interest rates, due dates, payments, your monthly budget and your settings — is saved **only on your phone**, in an encrypted database. The encryption key is kept in your phone's secure storage.

Kash never asks for card numbers, CVV codes, expiry dates or online banking logins.

## What Kash sends over the internet
Nothing. Kash works fully in airplane mode. There is no sign-up, no tracking, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting.

The one exception, once Kash Pro is on sale: if you buy or restore Pro, your phone talks to **Google Play** to handle the purchase. Google handles your payment details under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Kash only remembers on your phone that Pro is unlocked.

## Backups
If you export a backup, Kash creates a file locked with a password you choose and opens your phone's share sheet. Where the file goes (Files, Google Drive, email…) is up to you, and that service's own privacy policy applies. Without your password nobody can open the file, including us. Android's automatic cloud backup of Kash's data is turned off.

## Reminders
Due-date and statement reminders are scheduled on your phone. They show only the debt's nickname, never amounts.

## Fingerprint and face unlock
These use your phone's built-in prompt. Kash never receives your fingerprint or face data.

## Deleting your data
Settings → Delete all data erases everything on this phone. Uninstalling Kash does the same. Because we never had your data, there's nothing for us to delete on our side.

## Children
Kash is meant for adults managing their own debts.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Kash will never start collecting data quietly; any change like that would be opt-in.
